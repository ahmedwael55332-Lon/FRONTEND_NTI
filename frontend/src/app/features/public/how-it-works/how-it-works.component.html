HOW IT WORKS

Simple workflows. Clear decisions.

The system keeps the core blood bank workflow focused on the information and operations supported by the backend.

01

Create an account

Register as a donor or requester and access the appropriate experience.

02

Manage your role

Donors manage availability and eligibility. Requesters manage their blood requests.

03

Match blood needs

Requests are matched using blood type and Rh compatibility.

04

Handle emergencies

Compatible donors can receive real-time emergency request notifications.
